# Biryani

## Ingredients
- Basmati rice
- Chicken
- Onion
- Tomato
- Ginger-garlic paste
- Biryani spices

## Preparation
1. Marinate the chicken with spices and yogurt.
2. Cook the rice until partially done.
3. Cook the chicken with onions, tomatoes, and spices.
4. Layer the rice and chicken, then cook until fully done.
